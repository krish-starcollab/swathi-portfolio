Overview

Dedicated to advancing evidence-based, sustainable pest management.

My research focuses on evaluating the comparative toxicity and dose-response kinetics of botanical derivatives, microbial entomopathogens, and chemical standards against major economic crop pests, particularly Spodoptera frugiperda (fall armyworm).

Through rigorous laboratory bioassays and field population correlation studies, I seek to design practical IPM frameworks that mitigate ecological hazards and preserve beneficial arthropods while maintaining agricultural productivity.
