Research Perspective

“Transitioning from broad-spectrum chemical reliance to precise, ecologically harmonized pest management systems through biological control and bio-rational chemistry.”

My work centres on evaluating the comparative toxicity and dose-response kinetics of microbial entomopathogens and botanical derivatives against devastating agricultural pests such as Spodoptera frugiperda (fall armyworm). Combining rigorous laboratory bioassays with field population dynamics, I aim to provide evidence-based IPM frameworks that safeguard agroecosystems without compromising yield.
