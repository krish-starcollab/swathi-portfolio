M.Sc. Postgraduate Thesis Research (2023 – 2025) MPKV Rahuri

Evaluation of toxicity of entomopathogens, pesticidal plants and chemicals against fall armyworm Spodoptera frugiperda (J. E. Smith)

Research Guide: Dr. A. D. Gonde, Assistant Professor of Entomology, Dr. SPCOA Baramati

Conducted comprehensive toxicological bioassays under laboratory conditions to assess comparative efficacy of microbial entomopathogens (fungal/bacterial formulations), botanical pesticidal plant extracts, and recommended chemical insecticides against invasive fall armyworm larvae. Utilized Probit analysis for determining LC50 / lethal concentration thresholds and mortality kinetics for integrated pest management (IPM) formulations.
